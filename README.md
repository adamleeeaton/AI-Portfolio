# AI Training for Student Success

> **Portfolio project notice:** This repository is an educational portfolio project created to demonstrate AI enablement, prompting, and responsible implementation practices. Examples are fictional or de-identified, and this project is not an official BYU-Idaho policy, product, or institutional endorsement.

## Overview

This repository documents a portfolio project focused on applying artificial intelligence (AI) to student success use cases inspired by academic advising, student support services, and institutional effectiveness at **Brigham Young University–Idaho (BYU-Idaho)**. It is intended to demonstrate how AI enablement, human-centered design, prompting, and workflow thinking can support student-facing work.

The project draws on principles such as student-centered learning, instructor-guided engagement, and meaningful student preparation. It explores how AI can responsibly augment these principles by enhancing human decision-making, expanding access to support, and enabling more personalized, scalable student experiences without replacing human judgment or relationships.

The effort is not purely technical; it is intentionally human-centered. It considers how technology can support the educational and developmental objectives of a university while preserving the human elements central to student success. Institutional claims, policies, and tool approvals should always be verified against current official sources.

## Objectives

* Improve student retention, persistence, and completion rates
* Enhance the quality and consistency of academic advising
* Increase access to timely, accurate student support
* Reduce administrative burden on staff through automation
* Enable data-informed decision-making at scale

---

## Key Focus Areas

### 1. AI in Academic Advising

* Intelligent advising assistants to support common student inquiries
* Degree planning and pathway optimization
* Early alert systems for at-risk students
* Advisor augmentation (not replacement)

### 2. Student Support Automation

* AI-powered chat and self-service tools
* Knowledge base integration and retrieval
* Case triage and routing
* 24/7 support availability

### 3. Data & Predictive Analytics

* Risk modeling for retention and academic performance
* Behavioral and engagement signal analysis
* Intervention timing optimization

### 4. Personalization

* Adaptive recommendations for courses and resources
* Context-aware student guidance
* Tailored communication strategies

### 5. Ethical & Responsible AI

* Data privacy and FERPA-aware safeguards
* Bias detection and mitigation
* Transparency and explainability
* Human-in-the-loop governance

## Guiding Principles

* **Student-Centered**: Prioritize student outcomes and experience in alignment with BYU-Idaho’s Learning Model
* **Augmentation Over Replacement**: Support advisors, faculty, and staff—preserving high-touch mentoring relationships
* **Responsible AI**: Use ethical, transparent, FERPA-aware safeguards and follow current institutional privacy requirements
* **Scalability**: Design solutions that can expand across campuses, programs, and modalities (on-campus and online)
* **Alignment with Mission**: Ensure all AI applications reinforce BYU-Idaho’s mission and spiritual, educational, and developmental objectives

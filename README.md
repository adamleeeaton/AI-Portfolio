# AI Enablement Portfolio

> This repository is a professional portfolio demonstrating applied AI, enablement, workflow improvement, and responsible adoption practices. Examples are generalized, fictional, or de-identified and are not official policy or guidance for any institution.

## Overview

This portfolio shows how I help people and organizations turn emerging AI capabilities into practical, measurable improvements. My work combines AI training, stakeholder engagement, process analysis, knowledge-base design, workflow automation, and human-centered change management.

The focus is not only on what an AI tool can do. It is also on whether people understand it, trust it, use it appropriately, and can connect it to the work they actually perform.

## What This Portfolio Demonstrates

- Designing and delivering AI enablement workshops for different levels of technical confidence
- Translating complex AI concepts into practical, role-specific workflows
- Building and refining custom AI assistants, reusable skills, and knowledge-base systems
- Teaching users to create custom AI solutions for training, onboarding, and process improvement
- Identifying automation opportunities with no-code, low-code, and light-code tools
- Coordinating subject-matter experts to improve documentation and response quality
- Measuring adoption, satisfaction, usage, and operational impact
- Applying human review, privacy safeguards, and responsible-use practices to AI workflows

## Responsible Use Principles

- Use only the information needed for the requested task.
- Do not include private student, employee, customer, or institutional data in public examples.
- Verify policies, deadlines, calculations, and other high-impact information against authoritative sources.
- Keep a human reviewer responsible for decisions affecting people.
- Use fictional or de-identified examples for demonstrations and training.
- Configure institution-specific safety, privacy, grade, referral, and escalation requirements before deployment.
- Treat AI output as a draft or decision-support input, not as an official determination.
